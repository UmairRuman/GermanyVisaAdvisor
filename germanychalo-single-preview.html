Frequently Asked Questions

Useful answers stay here instead of crowding the homepage.

Can I study in Germany with a low CGPA?＋

It depends on the university and program. Suitable shortlisting and overall profile matter.

Do I need IELTS?＋

Requirements vary. Some English-taught programs require IELTS or another accepted proof of English.

Can I study in Germany for free?＋

Many public universities have low or no tuition for many programs, but semester contributions and living costs still apply.

How much money do I need?＋

Plan for financial proof, fees, insurance, housing, travel and setup costs.

How long does the visa process take?＋

Processing times vary. Check current official embassy guidance for the latest information.
